001

1. Subject + Verb

Hindi concept: Koi kaam/action karta hai.

Present: I work. / Main kaam karta hoon.
Past: I worked. / Maine kaam kiya.
Future: I will work. / Main kaam karunga.
002

2. Subject + Verb + Object

Hindi concept: Karta + kya/ kise.

Present: I read books. / Main kitabein padhta hoon.
Past: I read a book. / Maine ek kitab padhi.
Future: I will read a book. / Main ek kitab padhunga.
003

3. Subject + be + Complement

Hindi concept: Kisi ki identity, condition ya state batana.

Present: She is happy. / Woh khush hai.
Past: She was happy. / Woh khush thi.
Future: She will be happy. / Woh khush hogi.
004

4. There is/are + noun

Hindi concept: Kisi cheez/person ke exist karne ki baat.

Present: There is a problem. / Ek samasya hai.
Past: There was a problem. / Ek samasya thi.
Future: There will be a problem. / Ek samasya hogi.
005

5. Subject + have/has + object

Hindi concept: Possession/relationship: kisi ke paas hona.

Present: I have a bike. / Mere paas bike hai.
Past: I had a bike. / Mere paas bike thi.
Future: I will have a bike. / Mere paas bike hogi.
006

6. Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

Hindi concept: Abhi chal raha action.

I am studying. / Main padh raha hoon.
They are working. / Ve kaam kar rahe hain.
007

7. Subject + was/were + V-ing

Hindi concept: Past mein action chal raha tha.

I was studying. / Main padh raha tha.
They were working. / Ve kaam kar rahe the.
008

8. Subject + will be + V-ing

Hindi concept: Future ke kisi time par action chal raha hoga.

I will be studying at 8. / Main 8 baje padh raha hounga.
They will be travelling tomorrow. / Ve kal travel kar rahe honge.
009

9. Subject + has/have + V3

Hindi concept: Past action ka present result/connection.

I have finished my work. / Maine apna kaam khatam kar liya hai.
She has arrived. / Woh pahunch gayi hai.
010

10. Subject + had + V3

Hindi concept: Past ke do actions mein jo pehle hua.

I had left before he came. / Uske aane se pehle main ja chuka tha.
She had finished dinner before 9. / 9 se pehle woh dinner kar chuki thi.
011

11. Subject + will have + V3

Hindi concept: Future ke ek point tak action complete ho chuka hoga.

I will have finished by 8. / 8 baje tak main khatam kar chuka hounga.
They will have arrived by tomorrow. / Kal tak ve pahunch chuke honge.
012

12. Subject + has/have been + V-ing

Hindi concept: Action past mein start hua aur ab tak continue hai.

I have been studying for two hours. / Main do ghante se padh raha hoon.
She has been working here since June. / Woh June se yahan kaam kar rahi hai.
013

13. Subject + had been + V-ing

Hindi concept: Past ke ek point tak action kuch time se chal raha tha.

I had been waiting for an hour. / Main ek ghante se wait kar raha tha.
He had been working all day. / Woh poore din kaam kar raha tha.
014

14. Subject + will have been + V-ing

Hindi concept: Future ke point tak action kitne time se chal raha hoga.

By 8, I will have been studying for three hours. / 8 baje tak mujhe padhte hue teen ghante ho jayenge.
Next month, she will have been working here for a year. / Agle mahine use yahan kaam karte hue ek saal ho jayega.
015

15. Subject + can/could + V1

Hindi concept: Ability/possibility; could = past ability ya softer request.

I can swim. / Main tair sakta hoon.
I could swim when I was ten. / Main das saal ki age mein tair sakta tha.
016

16. Subject + should + V1

Hindi concept: Advice ya expectation.

You should study. / Tumhe padhna chahiye.
You should have studied. / Tumhe padh lena chahiye tha.
017

17. Subject + must + V1

Hindi concept: Strong necessity/obligation.

You must wear a helmet. / Tumhe helmet zaroor pehna chahiye.
You must have heard this. / Tumne yeh zaroor suna hoga.
018

18. Subject + may/might + V1

Hindi concept: Possibility; might usually weaker.

It may rain. / Baarish ho sakti hai.
He might come tomorrow. / Shayad woh kal aaye.
019

19. Subject + have to + V1

Hindi concept: Majboori/requirement.

I have to leave. / Mujhe jaana padega.
I had to leave. / Mujhe jaana pada.
020

20. Subject + used to + V1

Hindi concept: Past ki habitual situation jo ab nahi hai.

I used to play cricket. / Main cricket khela karta tha.
She used to live here. / Woh yahan raha karti thi.
021

21. be supposed to + V1

Hindi concept: Expected/duty/plan.

You are supposed to be here. / Tumse yahan hone ki expectation hai.
We were supposed to meet yesterday. / Humein kal milna tha.
022

22. want/need/plan/decide + to + V1

Hindi concept: Kisi action ki desire, need, plan ya decision.

I want to learn English. / Main English seekhna chahta hoon.
I decided to start today. / Maine aaj shuru karne ka faisla kiya.
023

23. enjoy/avoid/keep + V-ing

Hindi concept: In verbs ke baad gerund (-ing) aata hai.

I enjoy learning English. / Mujhe English seekhna pasand hai.
Keep practising. / Practice karte raho.
024

24. stop + V-ing / stop + to V1

Hindi concept: Stop doing = action band karna; stop to do = kisi aur kaam ke liye rukna.

I stopped smoking. / Maine smoking chhod di.
I stopped to drink water. / Main paani peene ke liye ruka.
025

25. in order to / so as to + V1

Hindi concept: Purpose: kisi kaam ko karne ka purpose.

I study in order to improve. / Main improve karne ke liye padhta hoon.
He left early so as to catch the bus. / Bus pakadne ke liye woh jaldi nikla.
026

26. Do/Does/Did + subject + V1?

Hindi concept: Simple tense ke questions.

Do you ride? / Kya tum ride karte ho?
Did you ride yesterday? / Kya tumne kal ride ki?
027

27. Wh-word + auxiliary + subject + verb?

Hindi concept: What/why/when/how etc. se detailed question.

Why do you study? / Tum kyun padhte ho?
Why did you leave? / Tum kyun gaye?
028

28. Embedded question: Could you tell me + wh-clause?

Hindi concept: Polite/formal question.

Could you tell me where he lives? / Kya aap bata sakte hain woh kahan rehta hai?
Could you tell me why she left? / Kya aap bata sakte hain woh kyun gayi?
029

29. Direct → reported speech

Hindi concept: Kisi ki baat ko indirectly batana.

He says, “I am busy.” → He says that he is busy. / Woh kehta hai ki woh busy hai.
He said, “I was busy.” → He said that he had been busy. / Usne kaha ki woh busy tha.
030

30. Noun + who/which/that + clause

Hindi concept: Noun ko describe karna.

The man who called me is my friend. / Jo aadmi mujhe call kar raha tha woh mera dost hai.
The bike that I bought is fast. / Jo bike maine kharidi woh fast hai.
031

31. The reason why + clause

Hindi concept: Reason explain karna.

The reason why I left is simple. / Mere jaane ka reason simple hai.
The reason why he left was unclear. / Uske jaane ka reason clear nahi tha.
032

32. What + clause + be

Hindi concept: Puri clause ko noun ki tarah use karke focus banana.

What I need is time. / Mujhe jo chahiye woh time hai.
What I wanted was an answer. / Mujhe jo chahiye tha woh ek answer tha.
033

33. Whatever/Whoever/Wherever + clause

Hindi concept: No matter what/who/where type meaning.

Whatever you choose, I will support you. / Tum jo bhi choose karo, main support karunga.
Wherever you go, stay safe. / Tum jahan bhi jao, safe rehna.
034

34. If + present, will + V1 (First conditional)

Hindi concept: Real/possible future condition.

If you study, you will improve. / Agar tum padhoge, improve karoge.
If it rains, we will stay home. / Agar baarish hui, hum ghar rahenge.
035

35. If + past, would + V1 (Second conditional)

Hindi concept: Unreal/hypothetical present/future.

If I had time, I would travel. / Agar mere paas time hota, main travel karta.
If I were you, I would wait. / Agar main tumhari jagah hota, wait karta.
036

36. If + had + V3, would have + V3 (Third conditional)

Hindi concept: Past ki unreal condition/regret.

If I had studied, I would have passed. / Agar maine padha hota, main pass ho gaya hota.
If she had left earlier, she would have caught the bus. / Agar woh pehle nikli hoti, bus pakad leti.
037

37. Mixed conditional

Hindi concept: Past condition ka present result ya present condition ka past result.

If I had slept earlier, I would feel better now. / Agar main pehle soya hota, ab better feel karta.
If I were more careful, I wouldn't have made that mistake. / Agar main zyada careful hota, woh mistake nahi ki hoti.
038

38. be + V3 (Passive)

Hindi concept: Focus action/receiver par.

The bike is repaired. / Bike repair ki jaati hai.
The bike was repaired yesterday. / Bike kal repair ki gayi thi.
039

39. modal + be + V3

Hindi concept: Must/can/should etc. ka passive.

The work must be finished. / Kaam zaroor khatam kiya jana chahiye.
The problem can be solved. / Problem solve ki ja sakti hai.
040

40. have/get + object + V3

Hindi concept: Kisi se apna kaam karwana.

I had my bike repaired. / Maine bike repair karwai.
I will get my phone fixed tomorrow. / Main kal phone repair karwaunga.
041

41. make/let/have + object + V1

Hindi concept: Kisi ko force/allow/instruct karke action karwana.

He made me wait. / Usne mujhe wait karne par majboor kiya.
She let me use her phone. / Usne mujhe apna phone use karne diya.
042

42. It is/was + focus + that/who...

Hindi concept: Cleft sentence: sentence ke ek part par strong focus.

It was John who helped me. / Meri help John ne hi ki thi.
It is this bike that I want. / Mujhe yehi bike chahiye.
043

43. What + clause + is/was...

Hindi concept: Focus/emphasis.

What I want is freedom. / Main jo chahta hoon woh freedom hai.
What I needed was confidence. / Mujhe jis cheez ki zarurat thi woh confidence tha.
044

44. The thing is, + clause

Hindi concept: Main point introduce karna.

The thing is, I don't agree. / Baat ye hai ki main agree nahi karta.
The thing was, we had no time. / Baat ye thi ki hamare paas time nahi tha.
045

45. Not only... but also...

Hindi concept: Do strong points ko emphasize karna.

He is not only smart but also disciplined. / Woh sirf smart hi nahi, disciplined bhi hai.
She not only helped me but also encouraged me. / Usne sirf help nahi ki, encourage bhi kiya.
046

46. What I mean is...

Hindi concept: Apni baat ko clarify/reframe karna.

What I mean is, we need a better plan. / Mera matlab hai humein better plan chahiye.
What I meant was that I needed time. / Mera matlab tha ki mujhe time chahiye tha.
047

47. Never/Rarely/Seldom + auxiliary + subject + V...

Hindi concept: Negative/limiting adverb ko front mein laakar formal emphasis.

Never have I seen such a view. / Maine aisa view kabhi nahi dekha.
Rarely does he make mistakes. / Woh bahut kam mistakes karta hai.
048

48. Only then/Only after... + auxiliary + subject...

Hindi concept: ‘Tabhi/ke baad hi’ par strong emphasis.

Only then did I understand. / Tabhi mujhe samajh aaya.
Only after he arrived did we start. / Uske aane ke baad hi humne shuru kiya.
049

49. Not until... + inversion

Hindi concept: ‘Tab tak nahi jab tak...’ strong formal pattern.

Not until midnight did he return. / Aadhi raat tak woh wapas nahi aaya.
Not until I tried it did I understand. / Jab tak maine try nahi kiya, mujhe samajh nahi aaya.
050

50. Had I known..., I would have...

Hindi concept: Formal third conditional without if.

Had I known, I would have helped. / Agar mujhe pata hota, main help karta.
Had she arrived earlier, she would have met him. / Agar woh pehle aayi hoti, usse mil leti.
051

51. Were I to..., I would...

Hindi concept: Formal hypothetical future.

Were I to start again, I would plan better. / Agar main dobara shuru karta, better plan karta.
Were he to ask me, I would agree. / Agar woh mujhse pooche, main agree karunga.
052

52. Should you need..., ...

Hindi concept: Formal polite condition.

Should you need help, call me. / Agar tumhe help chahiye ho, call karna.
Should you have any questions, let me know. / Agar koi question ho, bata dena.
053

53. V-ing..., main clause

Hindi concept: Same subject ke simultaneous/related action ko compact banana.

Walking home, I called him. / Ghar jaate hue maine use call kiya.
Knowing the truth, she stayed silent. / Sach jaankar woh chup rahi.
054

54. V3/Being + V3..., main clause

Hindi concept: Passive/reduced clause; formal writing.

Surprised by the news, he left. / News se surprised hokar woh chala gaya.
Being tired, she went to bed. / Thaki hui hone ke karan woh so gayi.
055

55. Having + V3..., main clause

Hindi concept: Pehle complete hua action, phir main action.

Having finished the work, I went home. / Kaam khatam karke main ghar gaya.
Having seen the message, she called me. / Message dekhne ke baad usne mujhe call kiya.
056

56. There is no point in + V-ing

Hindi concept: Kisi action ka koi fayda nahi.

There is no point in arguing. / Bahas karne ka koi fayda nahi.
There was no point in waiting. / Wait karne ka koi fayda nahi tha.
057

57. It is worth + V-ing

Hindi concept: Kisi kaam ka value/fayda hai.

It is worth trying. / Try karna worth hai.
It was worth waiting. / Wait karna worth tha.
058

58. It takes + person + time + to V1

Hindi concept: Kisi action mein kitna time lagta hai.

It takes me an hour to study. / Mujhe padhne mein ek ghanta lagta hai.
It took me two days to finish it. / Mujhe ise finish karne mein do din lage.
059

59. No matter + wh-word...

Hindi concept: Chahe kuch bhi ho, result same.

No matter what happens, I will stay calm. / Chahe jo ho, main calm rahunga.
No matter how hard it was, I continued. / Chahe kitna bhi hard tha, main continue karta raha.
060

60. As if / as though + clause

Hindi concept: Aise jaise...

He talks as if he knows everything. / Woh aise baat karta hai jaise sab jaanta ho.
She acted as though nothing had happened. / Usne aise behave kiya jaise kuch hua hi na ho.
061

61. The more..., the more...

Hindi concept: Jitna..., utna...

The more you practise, the better you become. / Jitna practice karoge, utne better banoge.
The more I learned, the more curious I became. / Jitna seekha, utni curiosity badhi.
062

62. So + adjective + that...

Hindi concept: Itna... ki...

It was so cold that we stayed inside. / Itni thand thi ki hum andar rahe.
He was so tired that he fell asleep. / Woh itna tired tha ki so gaya.
063

63. Such + noun + that...

Hindi concept: Aisa/itna + noun ki...

It was such a good movie that I watched it twice. / Film itni achhi thi ki maine do baar dekhi.
He is such a helpful person that everyone trusts him. / Woh itna helpful insaan hai ki sab trust karte hain.
064

64. By the time + clause

Hindi concept: Jab tak..., tab tak...

By the time you arrive, I will have left. / Jab tak tum aaoge, main ja chuka hounga.
By the time he came, we had finished. / Jab tak woh aaya, hum finish kar chuke the.
065

65. Despite/In spite of + noun/V-ing

Hindi concept: Despite = bawajood.

Despite being tired, I worked. / Thaka hone ke bawajood maine kaam kiya.
Despite the rain, we went out. / Baarish ke bawajood hum bahar gaye.
066

66. Whereas/While + clause

Hindi concept: Do situations ka contrast.

I like tea, whereas he likes coffee. / Mujhe chai pasand hai, jabki use coffee.
While I wanted to stay, she wanted to leave. / Main rukna chahta tha, jabki woh jaana chahti thi.
067

67. Provided/Providing that + clause

Hindi concept: Condition: basharte ki.

You can go provided that you finish the work. / Kaam finish karo, basharte tum ja sakte ho.
We will help provided that you cooperate. / Hum help karenge, agar tum cooperate karo.
068

68. Unless + clause

Hindi concept: If not = jab tak nahi/agar nahi.

I won't go unless you come. / Main nahi jaunga jab tak tum nahi aate.
She won't pass unless she studies. / Jab tak woh nahi padhegi, pass nahi hogi.
069

69. It is high time + subject + past

Hindi concept: Ab karna hi chahiye; present meaning but past form.

It is high time you started. / Ab tumhe shuru kar dena chahiye.
It is high time we left. / Ab humein nikal jana chahiye.
070

70. I wish + past / had + V3

Hindi concept: Present wish ya past regret.

I wish I were taller. / Kaash main lamba hota.
I wish I had studied harder. / Kaash maine zyada padhai ki hoti.
071

71. The fact that + clause

Hindi concept: Kisi fact ko noun phrase ki tarah use karna.

The fact that you tried matters. / Tumne try kiya, ye fact important hai.
The fact that he had left surprised me. / Uske chale jaane ki baat ne mujhe surprise kiya.
072

72. What matters is/was...

Hindi concept: Sabse important cheez batana.

What matters is your effort. / Important tumhari effort hai.
What mattered was that we stayed together. / Important ye tha ki hum saath rahe.
073

73. As far as I know...

Hindi concept: Jitna mujhe pata hai.

As far as I know, he lives here. / Jitna mujhe pata hai, woh yahan rehta hai.
As far as I knew, the plan was cancelled. / Jitna mujhe pata tha, plan cancel tha.
074

74. From what I understand...

Hindi concept: Available information ke basis par opinion.

From what I understand, the meeting is tomorrow. / Meri understanding ke hisaab se meeting kal hai.
From what I understood, he had already left. / Meri samajh ke mutabik woh ja chuka tha.
075

75. Having said that,...

Hindi concept: Pehli baat ko acknowledge karke contrast/next point.

It is expensive. Having said that, it is useful. / Mehenga hai, lekin useful hai.
He was tired. Having said that, he continued working. / Woh tired tha, phir bhi kaam karta raha.
076

76. That being said,...

Hindi concept: Formal contrast/qualification.

The plan is risky. That being said, it may work. / Plan risky hai, lekin kaam kar sakta hai.
The job was difficult. That being said, I learned a lot. / Job difficult thi, lekin maine bahut seekha.
